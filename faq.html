Frequently Asked Questions

Last updated: October 2026

Getting started

What is CirkleUp?

CirkleUp is an invite-only social platform where creators share content, host ticketed events and build a community around their work.

How do I get access?

Join the waitlist at cirkleup.com. We release invites in batches every Monday. You can also join if an existing member invites you. Invite codes for waitlist members arrive by email. Once you have a code, download the app from the App Store or Google Play, enter your code and create your account. You must be at least 13 to join.

How many invites do I get?

Every new member starts with 5 invites to share with people they want in their circle. Each invite code can be used once. Your invite allowance can grow as you become a more established member of the community.

Earning and payouts

How much do creators keep?

Creators keep 90%* of what they earn. CirkleUp keeps a flat 10% platform fee.

*New members start with a 30-day grace period (see the next question). It currently applies to event ticket sales.

What is the referral bonus and the 30-day grace period?

For a new member's first 30 days after signing up, each ticket sale is split 80% to the creator, 10% to the member who directly invited them (the referral bonus), and 10% to CirkleUp. After 30 days, the split returns to 90% creator and 10% CirkleUp, and the referral bonus ends. The bonus is paid only to the person who directly invited the creator. It does not extend to anyone further along the chain, and it currently applies to event ticket sales only.

How and when do I get paid for ticket sales?

Ticket earnings are held as pending until your event has taken place. Once the event is completed, they move to your earned balance. Withdrawals are not available yet. We are building them next, and we will publish how they work (method, minimums, timing and any fees) here and in the app before they launch.

What happens if an event is cancelled?

A host can cancel an event. When they do, every attendee is automatically refunded the Liberty Coins they spent, and the host's earnings from those tickets are reversed. Because ticket earnings are held until the event is completed, refunds can always be issued cleanly.

What are Liberty Coins?

Liberty Coins are the in-app currency. You can buy coin packs in the app through the App Store or Google Play, and spend coins on event tickets and other paid features. Experience points (XP) are separate and cannot be bought, cashed out or converted to coins. Purchased coins have no cash value. Withdrawing earnings is not available yet.

Are purchases refundable?

All purchases are non-refundable by default. Refunds may be honored in certain circumstances, such as event cancellations.

Safety and account

How do I report a problem or inappropriate content?

Use the in-app report function on posts, comments and profiles, and you can block any user from their profile or from Account Settings → Blocked users. Blocked users can no longer interact with you. Reports are reviewed by our team, who can remove content and suspend or ban accounts. For urgent safety concerns, email info@cirkleup.com. See our Child Safety Standards.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account in the app under Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. If you can't access the app, email info@cirkleup.com from the address on your account and we will process your request within 7 business days.

How do I contact support?

Email info@cirkleup.com. We aim to respond within 24–48 hours. More on our Support page.
