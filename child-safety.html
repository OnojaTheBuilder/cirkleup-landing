Child Safety Standards Policy

Published by CirkleUp · Updated October 2026

CirkleUp has zero tolerance for content or behavior that exploits, endangers or harms children and young people. This policy applies to every user, post and interaction on the platform.

CirkleUp supports the child safety requirements of Google Play and the App Store. This page explains who can use the app and the tools and rules we use to keep young people safe.

Who can use CirkleUp

CirkleUp is for people aged 13 and older, and joining is by invitation only. Users enter their date of birth when they sign up. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone under 13, and we will remove accounts we learn belong to children under 13.

Reporting

Users can report posts, comments and profiles directly in the app. Reports go to our moderation team, who review them and can remove content and suspend or ban accounts. We prioritize reports that involve children.

Blocking

Users can block other users from the profile or from Account Settings → Blocked users. Blocked users can no longer interact with the person who blocked them.

Content we do not allow

  • Child sexual abuse material and any sexualized content involving minors, including sexual roleplay
  • Grooming, predatory or exploitative contact with minors
  • Pornographic and explicit sexual content
  • Graphic violence, gore, torture and extreme cruelty
  • Hate speech, bullying and harassment
  • Dangerous challenges, and content that promotes drug use, self-harm, suicide or eating disorders
  • Gambling, betting and any other illegal content

We remove content that breaks these rules and may permanently ban the accounts responsible.

Grooming and child exploitation: zero tolerance

If we suspect grooming or exploitation, our team reviews the reported behavior and restricts or bans the account. Confirmed cases lead to a permanent ban, and we report them to law enforcement and, where required, to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC).

Account deletion

Users can delete their account in the app under Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account. See our Privacy Policy for what happens to data after deletion.

Our commitments

  • Review and act on reports of policy violations
  • Review these standards regularly and update them as the app grows

Further information

For more on child safety standards, see the official Google Play Developer Policy page and the App Store Review Guidelines.

Contact

To report a safety concern or ask about this policy, email info@cirkleup.com.
